Setup, error and out-of-date states

One design for every widget, always under the widget's title. Blue means something to set up, with where to do it. Red means it couldn't get data and nothing is on screen yet, and says when it will try again. Amber means a refresh failed but the last good data is still shown, marked with how old it is (here, readings from 3:58 PM).
